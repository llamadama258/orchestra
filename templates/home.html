Frequently asked questions

Melodra supports PDF sheet music (via optical music recognition), MusicXML (.xml, .musicxml), and compressed MusicXML (.mxl) files. For best results, we recommend exporting directly as MusicXML from notation software like MuseScore, Finale, or Sibelius.

Yes! Melodra is completely free. Simply open the app, upload your sheet music, and start listening. No account or sign-up required.

Melodra uses Audiveris, a state-of-the-art optical music recognition (OMR) engine. Accuracy depends on scan quality — 300 DPI or higher works best. For the most accurate results, use MusicXML files exported directly from notation software.
