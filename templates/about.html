About Melodra

Making music accessible to everyone

We believe every musician deserves to hear their music — not just read it. Melodra bridges the gap between the written score and the sound it represents.

Our Story

Born from a rehearsal room problem

Melodra was created by orchestra musicians who were tired of showing up to rehearsal without ever having heard their part. Not everyone has access to a pianist, and MIDI playback from notation software always sounded robotic and lifeless.

We built Melodra to solve that. Upload any sheet music — even a scanned PDF — and hear it played back with realistic instrument samples. No setup, no subscriptions, no friction. Just music.

Our commitment is to keep Melodra free and open, making music education more accessible to students, teachers, and performers everywhere.

Our Mission

Bridging written music and audio

Melodra specifically empowers orchestra musicians and music educators by turning static scores into dynamic, listenable performances.

🎧

Hear Before You Play

Students and performers can listen to their parts before rehearsal, building familiarity with rhythm, pitch, and phrasing.

📚

Teaching Tool

Educators use Melodra to demonstrate parts, compare arrangements, and help students understand how their voice fits in the ensemble.

🌏

Accessible Everywhere

Melodra runs in any browser with no installation. Students in any setting can access it — all they need is their sheet music.
